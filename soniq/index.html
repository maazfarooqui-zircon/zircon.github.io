MULTIROTOR · 4 ROTORS
QUADCOPTER
Four open rotors in X or + configuration. Most common urban air mobility platform. Alternating CW/CCW pairs cancel torque. Moderate acoustic footprint.
4 ROTORS VTOL HOVER-OPT 2–3 BLADE
MULTIROTOR · 6 ROTORS
HEXACOPTER
Six-rotor platform offering redundancy and higher payload. Wider acoustic footprint but better fault tolerance. Common in commercial UAM.
6 ROTORS REDUNDANT HIGH PAYLOAD
COAXIAL · CONTRA-ROTATING
COAXIAL ROTOR
Two contra-rotating rotors on a single shaft. Compact footprint with torque cancellation. Unique BVI interaction between upper and lower rotors increases tonal noise complexity.
2 ROTORS CONTRA-ROT BVI NOISE
TANDEM · LONGITUDINAL
TANDEM ROTOR
Front and rear rotors on a conventional fuselage — helicopter-style layout. Excellent range/payload ratio. Fore-aft acoustic interference creates characteristic noise pattern.
2 ROTORS LONG RANGE INTERFERENCE
HYBRID · LIFT + CRUISE
LIFT+CRUISE
Dedicated lift rotors for hover/VTOL and a pusher/puller propeller for cruise. Most efficient for longer ranges. Rotors tilt or fold in cruise — complex acoustic transition modes.
3+ ROTORS RANGE OPT PUSHER PROP
MULTIROTOR · 8 ROTORS
OCTOCOPTER
Eight rotors for maximum redundancy. Heavy-lift UAM platform. Combined noise from 8 sources creates complex spectral interactions. Higher overall SPL than smaller configurations.
8 ROTORS MAX REDUND. HEAVY LIFT
