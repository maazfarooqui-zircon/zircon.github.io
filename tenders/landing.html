Designed Exclusively for Indian Government Procurement

ZirconIQ is purpose-built for contractors, suppliers and consultants competing in India's public sector — from Maharatna PSUs to Central Government departments to State utilities.

Every piece of intelligence is specific to India's procurement landscape — CPWD DSR rates, eprocure.gov.in, GeM, NIC portals, CVC guidelines and India-specific contractor behaviour.

  • All 11 Maharatna PSUs — NTPC, ONGC, BHEL, IOCL, Coal India, Power Grid, GAIL, SAIL, BPCL, HPCL, HAL
  • Central Govt departments — CPWD, MES, AAI, AIIMS, ESIC, NHM, NIC, C-DAC, MeitY, NPCI
  • State utilities — UPPCL, TANGEDCO, MSEDCL, KSEB, WBSEDCL and renewable agencies
  • DSR 2023 rates for CPWD and MES — Civil, Electrical and Horticulture
  • India-specific contractor profiles — KEC, Kalpataru, L&T, Afcons, TCS, Wipro, GE Healthcare
  • All pricing in ₹ INR with Crore denomination throughout
