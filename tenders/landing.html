Margin Intelligence

Not All Government
Work Pays the Same.

ZirconIQ tells you which tender types deliver the best margin — and which destroy it. Bid smarter, not just harder.

Electrical Works
⚡
EHV Cable Laying
8–12%
Net margin — limited competition
Few contractors are qualified. PSU estimate is usually realistic. AMC contracts after completion yield 15–20% margin with almost no competition.
⚠ Supply-only cable tenders: near-zero margin
Healthcare
🏥
Biomedical AMC
20–25%
Post-warranty AMC — almost no competition
Once a hospital's warranty expires, only a handful of companies can service that equipment. Margins are excellent and contracts renew automatically.
⚠ Consumables supply: only 3–5% margin
IT & Technology
💻
Custom Software Dev
18–25%
Hard to benchmark — PSU cannot compare
Custom software is the hardest type of tender to benchmark. PSUs cannot compare proposals like they compare civil or electrical. Price in scope creep buffer.
⚠ IT AMC/Hardware supply: only 5–8% margin
Structural & Fabrication
⚒️
Equipment Skids & Modules
12–18%
Modular fabrication — specialised
Equipment skids and modular assemblies require engineering capability that most contractors lack. Margin comes from steel procurement efficiency and design optimisation.
⚠ Generic fabrication: only 4–7% margin
O&M / AMC
🔧
Technical O&M Services
12–20%
Multi-year contracts — recurring revenue
Technical O&M requires skilled staff that most small contractors lack. Multi-year contracts with annual escalation clauses are the most profitable category in PSU tendering.
⚠ Housekeeping & security: only 3–5% margin
Environment & Green
🌿
ETP/STP Design-Build
10–15%
Specialised — environmental licence needed
Water and effluent treatment requires specialist qualifications most contractors don't have. Hazardous waste is 15–20% but needs pollution control licences.
⚠ Solar rooftop EPC: now 4–7% (commoditised)
